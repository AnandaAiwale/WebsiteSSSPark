Simple Workflow

How it works in 3 easy steps

No complicated setup or IT training required. Anyone in your office can process attendance in under 60 seconds.

01

Download Logs to USB

Insert a standard USB pen drive into your biometric machine. Tap 'Download Attendance Logs' on the machine screen to save the log file (USB or spreadsheet format).

💡 Takes under 5 seconds on all standard biometric devices.
02

Plug & Click Import

Plug the USB pen drive into your laptop or desktop PC, open SmartBiometric Suite, and click Import Pen Drive. The software instantly parses and verifies all punches.

⚡ Automatically creates profiles for newly enrolled staff IDs.
03

View & Export Payslips

Review daily turnout, inspect late arrivals or overtime, generate the monthly 1-to-31 days muster roll, and export one-click PDF salary slips and Excel reports.

📲 Share payslips directly to employees on WhatsApp.
