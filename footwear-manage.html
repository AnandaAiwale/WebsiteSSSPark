Why We Built This App

5 Core Problems Solved for Footwear Retailers

Standard retail billing software fails footwear stores because shoes have unique sizing and pair challenges. Here is how Smart Footwear Shop Manager solves them.

01
Stock Precision

Shoe Size & Pair Variant Matrix

Footwear cannot be sold by plain quantity. Our specialized matrix tracks exact pairs across UK/India sizes 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and EU sizes. Instantly know if size 8 in white sneaker is in stock before making customers wait!

02
Zero Cost Hardware

Smartphone Camera Barcode Scanner

Eliminate costly laser guns and cumbersome desktop computers. Use your Android smartphone's camera to scan shoe box barcodes in less than half a second for instant billing.

03
Pocket Printing

ESC/POS Bluetooth Thermal Printing

Connects effortlessly to all budget 58mm & 80mm wireless Bluetooth thermal printers. Print professional bills with your shop name, shoe size, pair price, GST, and size exchange policy.

04
Fast Payments

Dynamic UPI QR On Every Bill

Print a dynamic UPI QR code on the receipt showing the exact bill amount. Customers scan and pay directly via GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm — reducing cash handling errors and counter rush.

05
Zero Downtime

100% Offline Standalone Architecture

No Wi-Fi, data pack, or cloud server dependency. Your billing never stops during internet outages, and your daily profits, sales margins, and supplier costs remain 100% private on your device.
