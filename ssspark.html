Opening script

SSSPark is a QR sticker for the vehicle. You attach it once. Anyone with a phone can scan it and see the details you choose to share, including a way to reach you. They do not install an app. It works for private cars, two-wheelers, taxis, and delivery vehicles.

If they ask what happens after they buy

  1. They purchase or register the sticker.
  2. A unique QR code is tied to that vehicle.
  3. They attach the sticker.
  4. Someone scans it and the SSSPark page shows the information set for that code.
